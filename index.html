Pail Math

The 315 apples at 8 apples a pail take 40 pails, the last pail holding 3 apples.

Boundary: arithmetic only Pail Math counts an event's orchard apples with multiplication, division and rounding-up: the pails the apples take, the weeks the stock lasts, the cost an apple, and the minutes the packing takes. It uses no named constants. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your apples, your pails or your venue and does not know whether the apples will really stay fresh, the supplier will run out or the price will hold, and it gives no verdict that a pail is worth its price or an apple is right for your event. Every number here is your own entry.

Starting examples: 315 apples, 8 apples a pail, 25 apples a week, $9.60 a pail and 4 apples a minute.

The weeks truth

The 315 apples at 25 apples a week last 12 full weeks with 15 apples left for the next week.

The cost truth

The 40 pails at $9.60 a pail come to $384.00, $1.22 an apple.

The time truth

The 315 apples at 4 apples a minute take 79 minutes, 1 hour 19 minutes.
